# OPAIJA Launch Roadmap

## Phase 1 - PayPal, Entitlements, and CRM Integrity

**Goal:** Every successful PayPal purchase issues the correct durable access and every consented lead enters a real delivery lifecycle.

**Requirements:** PAY-01 through PAY-05, GRW-01, GRW-02

**Success criteria:** Server-priced orders, verified/idempotent capture, durable entitlements, receipt delivery status, and no false success when providers are absent.

## Phase 2 - Public Funnel and Member Product

**Goal:** The complete free-reader to paid-member journey is coherent, responsive, asset-backed, and free of customer-facing placeholders.

**Requirements:** FUN-01 through FUN-04, MEM-01 through MEM-03

**Success criteria:** Mobile/desktop journey works, all visible CTAs perform real actions, unavailable offers are hidden, and member actions persist.

## Phase 3 - Twice-Daily SEO, GEO, and CRO Engine

**Goal:** OPAIJA maintains two high-quality public posts daily and exposes observable publishing health.

**Requirements:** GRW-03 through GRW-05

**Success criteria:** Durable slots, unique useful posts, public visibility rules, sitemap output, failure status, and admin observability.

## Phase 4 - Launch Verification and Operations

**Goal:** Produce evidence that the commercial system is safe and usable before calling it complete.

**Requirements:** OPS-01 through OPS-04

**Success criteria:** Build, API, browser, security, and production-config checks provide explicit pass/fail evidence.
