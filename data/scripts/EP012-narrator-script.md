# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

Some debts cannot wait. Some rhythms cannot be silenced.

At Mother Lall's stall, the flour-dust settled. She wrapped six orders. One for each of them. She placed the last in Kai's hand like a blessing she had been saving all his life.

Nobody spoke. Six papers. Six warriors. One supper before the storm.

Jabari broke the quiet. "This is it, right?" Kai looked at the bois. "This is it."

Then Selah came with her father's message. Come to the gayelle. Or every bois in Trinidad goes quiet. Forever.

They turned to find Malik already gone. He had been training alone. He did not ask. He never does.

Kai found him at the gate. No words. Two rivals. Two bois. One door.

Inside, the arena shifted. Marius Vale was already waiting.

"I said one drum. I meant it."

The Silence Pulse opened its mouth.

And across Trinidad, every drum forgot how to speak.
