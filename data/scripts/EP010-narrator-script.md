# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

Some men doh want to win. They want to erase.

Kai went looking for doubles before the show. Mother Lall's stall — closed. The next stall — closed. Every vendor on the road, gone. Brown paper bags flapping in a wind that did not feel like Trinidad.

Full belly. Steady spirit. Tonight, the belly stayed empty. The team felt it before they saw it. An empty stall is a quiet warning. A road of empty stalls is a verdict.

Inside the gathering, the music started. Pan, voice, drum, all braided together. Then, between one beat and the next, the song folded. Not stopped. Folded. Like cloth pressed flat by a hand that had been waiting.

On the stage, a man stood still. No shout. No movement. Only certainty.

Marius Vale.

Black cracks crawled up the speakers. The drums went quiet without permission. He let the silence finish for him.

Then his eyes found Kai in the crowd. Not searching. Recognising.

The drum the boy carried suddenly felt very small.
