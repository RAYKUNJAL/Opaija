# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

The oldest things always know the most.

Papa Etienne's house breathes wood. Carved cane. Carved wall. Carved memory.

Kai came for an answer. Why him. Why this stick.

The old man did not speak first. He set down a plate of doubles. Old recipe. Stronger.

"My mother used to make this before a fight."

Then he began.

They thought they took every weapon. They counted every blade. They missed the drum pegs. The walking canes. The hair beads. The pestle. The toy. The song.

The bois was broken on purpose. Hidden inside everyday things. And the lavway — the call and the answer — carried the wake-up word across the water, across generations, mouth to mouth, until a boy could find it again.

The Listening Bois glowed along every carving. Confirming. Remembering.

Nia did not hum. She listened.

Then Etienne looked at Kai. Steady. Tired. Honest.

"Marius was a Guardian too. Once."
