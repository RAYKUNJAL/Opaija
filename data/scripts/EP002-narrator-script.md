# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

The gayelle is not a ring. It is a memory.

Morning came soft over East Trinidad. The old fighting ground was empty. The dust still remembered last night's feet.

Kai Baptiste came back to give the stick away.

He found a doubles stall before he found his courage. Two orders. Bara warm, channa heavy. His hands would not stop shaking.

Nia Toussaint sat down beside him without a word. She did not look at Kai. She looked at the wood.

"Doh sing it unless yuh mean it," she said. Then softer: "But this stick already singing."

Kai tried to set the bois down on the ground. The bois would not stay. It came back to his hand like breath comes back to a chest.

Across the road, an old man watched. Cane in one hand. Eyes that had seen this stick before.

Papa Etienne Roach spoke seven words.

"That stick chose last time two hundred years ago."

He turned. He walked away.

The bois pulsed once. The gayelle remembered.
