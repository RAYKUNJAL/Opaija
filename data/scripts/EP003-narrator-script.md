# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

Not every rhythm wants company.

In the community gayelle, afternoon sun pressed flat against the dust. Kai Baptiste stood at the edge, paper in hand, doubles half-eaten. Full belly. Steady spirit. That was still his rule.

Across the ring, Malik St. Hill watched. He saw the food. He saw the boy. His lip curled at both.

Word had already travelled. The Listening Bois had chosen Kai. Malik St. Hill did not believe in chosen.

Malik stepped into the circle. The challenge needed no words.

The first exchange was a lesson. Malik moved like discipline made flesh — every strike on time, every block earned. Kai gave ground. He gave more ground. The bois in his hand felt heavy, untaught.

Then Jabs began to drum. Wooden. African. The L-shaped sticks finding their old, old shape.

And panic woke the Listening Bois. A pulse rolled out from Kai's feet. The gayelle ground shook.

Malik stepped back. Slowly. The contempt was gone from his eyes. Something colder had taken its place. Something that was thinking.
