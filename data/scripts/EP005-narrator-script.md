# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

History does leave marks. Most people just stop looking.

Asha Singh-Baptiste never stopped. Her room is paper and patience. Maps. Photographs. The margins of every page filled in her own hand.

She opened a notebook and showed Kai a different kind of archive. Every doubles vendor in Trinidad. Every route. Every stall. Mother Lall circled twice.

Kai went quiet. Delighted. A little afraid.

Then Asha laid down a photograph from 1885. An elder. Straight back. A bois in his grip.

The same bois.

Nia leaned in. Jabs lowered his phone. The room held its breath.

Asha touched the photograph, and for one second her eyes went somewhere else. Memory Sight. She saw the elder's hands as if they were her own.

The Listening Bois answered. A carving surfaced in the wood that nobody had carved.

Asha looked at the new mark. She looked at Kai.

"This means someone is coming. Someone old."
