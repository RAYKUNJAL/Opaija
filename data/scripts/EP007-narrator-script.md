# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

One drum. One command. And the gayelle ground forgot how to sing.

In Trinidad, the circle was full. Bois cracking. Lavway rising. Then — a young boishman dropped to one knee. His stick in his hand. His stick gone quiet.

Kai stepped to Mother Lall's stall, eyes on the boy, hand reaching for doubles.

Mother Lall stopped his hand.

She looked at him long. Wrapped the doubles in paper. Pressed it into his palm. "Take it with you. Don't eat it yet."

She had never said that before.

Asha knelt beside the boy. Read his pulse. Read the silence. "Somebody walked through here," she said. "Silence Pulse. Fresh."

Tariq turned his face to the wind. Tasted west. "Deliberate. Aimed."

The drums kept time. The gayelle held its breath.

And on the hill above the city, against a low and burning sky — a stillness. The back of a man. A drum sitting beside him like a kept promise.

He did not turn.

He did not need to.
