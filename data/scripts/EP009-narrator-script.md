# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

The quietest thing in the room is always the most dangerous.

The team will not eat tonight. Tension sits at the table where doubles should be. So Kai walks alone. Mother Lall hands him the paper without a word, then sits beside him. Two old rules between them. No questions. No comfort. Just curry and breath.

Across the city, Asha turns one photograph, then another, then a third. The watching girl has a name now. Daughter of a Guardian who vanished. Daughter of a debt no one finished paying.

By the waterfront, Nia stands alone. The girl arrives without footsteps. No bois. No hand raised. Only one sentence.

"Your lavway is wasted on them."

Nia does not answer with a song. She answers with stillness. The girl smiles, slow, like she knew the answer before she asked.

Then her pendant wakes. The same carving Kai wears at his chest. Turned the wrong way. Cold light where his is warm.

Silence has a name now.
