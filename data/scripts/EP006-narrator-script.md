# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

Some people move like water. You don't see them until they're already past you.

Port of Spain waterfront. Five fighters. One drum. One voice. One quiet rival. The team finally whole — or so they thought.

A shadow had been following them since Carnival. Not a sound. Not a scent. Just a missing beat where a footstep should be.

He stepped out of the noon glare like he had walked across the sea itself. Locs wrapped in teal. Shells in his hair. A bois of driftwood and ocean glass.

Kai pushed a hot doubles into the stranger's hand before a name was spoken. The boy stared at the bara like it was foreign country.

"You never had doubles before?"

"Tariq Davidson. Tobago. The Listening Bois woke me from across the water."

Then he was gone. Mid-sentence. Mid-breath. Behind Malik before Malik could turn.

"That doh mean allyuh own the whole sea."

He looked at Kai. The sea already moving. The silence moving faster.

You ready?
