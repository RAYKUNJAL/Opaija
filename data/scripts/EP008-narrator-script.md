# Narrator script (The Web-Teller)

Some blood does not spill. Some blood lives inside the wood.

The gayelle does not care who you are. Only what you carry.

Before the chalk circle, Kai unwrapped the doubles Mother Lall pressed into his hand. The pepper hit different tonight. Sharper. Like the old woman knew.

Full belly. Steady spirit. He stepped in.

Jabari raised the L-stick. The drum opened the floor. Nia's lavway laid the rhythm down like a road.

The opponent moved well. Too well. And under every strike, Kai heard something that did not belong — a thin, cold thread pulling the beat the wrong way. Silence, hiding inside rhythm.

Kai did not panic. He listened past it. He found his own pulse and rode it home. One last strike. The Listening Bois answered.

He won. Barely.

Then the crowd went quiet — not in cheer. In recognition. They had felt it too.

In the back row, a girl watched. Cold eyes. Pale gold fading at her wrists. She turned and walked away.
