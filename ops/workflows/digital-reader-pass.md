# Opaija Digital Reader Pass

## Purpose

Use the website to move fans from free previews into a paid digital library for Opaija comics, manga chapters, storybooks, artbooks, and printable books.

## Funnel

1. Public landing page shows hero video, story hook, characters, and flipbook sample.
2. Email gate unlocks the full character preview and founder giveaway.
3. Founder emails receive free sample pages and release alerts.
4. Paid reader pass opens when the first long-form book is ready.
5. Paid members get full chapters, bonus pages, lore drops, and early print/KDP notices.

## Product Length Targets

- Digital comic chapter: 32-48 pages.
- Manga chapter: 48-64 pages.
- Coloring book: 48-72 pages.
- Storybook: 24-40 pages.
- Artbook: 64-96 pages.

## Reader Requirements

- Free samples remain visible on the public site.
- Full books require member access after payment integration.
- Download and read events must be logged.
- Artwork should be watermarked for previews and protected from casual copying.
- Final PDFs must be separately checked for KDP trim, bleed, gutter, and cover-template requirements.

## Checkout Path

Stripe is the recommended first provider for the paid pass because it supports subscriptions, one-time purchases, webhooks, and customer portals. Add it after the Vault and reader library are stable.

## Agents

- Story Format Writer: builds the long-form script/page plan.
- Anime Action Director: designs fight-scene page energy.
- Paperclip Publisher: turns page plan into reader/KDP packets.
- Digital Comics Pass Manager: owns offer, pricing, access, and retention.
- Lore Guardian: approves canon and cultural consistency.
