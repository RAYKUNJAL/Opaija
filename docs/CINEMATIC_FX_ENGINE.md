# OPAIJA Cinematic FX Engine

## Decision

Add a specialized FX agent team now. Do not train a custom model first.

Training/fine-tuning only becomes worth it after we have enough approved Opaija action frames and can prove that normal reference-prompt workflows are drifting too much.

## FX Agents

- Cinematic FX Director: power effects, impact frames, particles, shockwaves, camera energy.
- Action Choreographer: anime fight beats, anticipation, smears, contact, recovery.
- Compositing Supervisor: Remotion layer order, glow, speed ramps, camera shake, captions, color grade.

## Opaija FX Pillars

- Pulse Energy: gold/orange rhythm rings and drumwave ripples.
- Voice/Lavway Command: gold script ribbons and bell pulses.
- Rootbreaker Force: red-black percussion cracks and vibration fractures.
- Memory Echo: teal/gold ghost frames and paper-sigil trails.
- Tidewatch Motion: sea-teal arcs, water flecks, cloth trails.

## Shot Standard

1. Lock silhouette and pose first.
2. Add power effects only after action remains readable.
3. Preserve face, hands, weapon, wardrobe, and character proportions.
4. Use Seedance for moving shots and Remotion for final compositing.
5. Store approved action frames as references for future scenes.
