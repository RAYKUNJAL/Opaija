# OPAIJA Tribe Growth Engine

## Goal

Build an owned fanbase fast while turning Opaija into a real media business: followers, email list, founder community, digital art drops, merch demand, book demand, ad audiences, and pilot-launch momentum.

## North Star

Owned email list first. Social platforms create reach, but email gives us a direct relationship with fans.

## Agent System

- Tribe Growth Commander: coordinates every channel and weekly growth review.
- Meta Ads Agent: Facebook/Instagram lead ads, retargeting, creative tests.
- TikTok Growth Agent: organic hook volume, Spark Ads candidates, creator-style posts.
- YouTube Growth Agent: Shorts, pilot packaging, thumbnails, subscriber conversion.
- Email Tribe Agent: lead capture, welcome sequence, fan tagging, newsletters.
- Story Blog Agent: lore/blog posts and lead magnet pages.
- Founder Drop Agent: early digital artwork gifts, founder pass, optional NFT path.

## Fast Growth Plan

### Week 1: Foundation

- Build domain landing page.
- Add email capture and founder art drop.
- Publish first 10 character reveal posts.
- Set up Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, GA4, and UTM rules.
- Create welcome email sequence.

### Week 2: Organic Volume

- Post 3-5 daily short-form assets across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and Facebook Reels.
- Launch story blog with lore and character posts.
- Release `The Gayelle Files` lead magnet.
- Pin email CTA on every post.

### Week 3: Paid Tests

- Launch Meta lead-gen campaign.
- Launch TikTok lead-gen or traffic campaign.
- Retarget video viewers and site visitors.
- Test 12 creatives: character reveals, FX scenes, founder art drop, lore mystery.

### Week 4: Tribe Conversion

- Launch founder pass waitlist.
- Run character popularity polls.
- Preview KDP comic/coloring pages.
- Poll first merch drops.
- Send weekly behind-the-scenes email.

### Week 5+: Scale

- Increase spend only on winning hooks.
- Build lookalike audiences from email leads.
- Launch first merch pre-drop.
- Publish pilot teaser comic/KDP sample.
- Keep weekly growth review with all agents.

## Lead Magnets

1. Founder Art Drop: digital artwork and behind-the-scenes style notes.
2. The Gayelle Files: illustrated story/lore/world intro.
3. Season 1 Character Pack: wallpapers and printable mini posters.
4. Founder Pass: access badge/perks; optional NFT later.

## NFT / Digital Collectible Position

Start with a digital founder collectible as a gift or access badge. Do not promise investment value. If we mint later, it should be framed as art, early supporter identity, and access to community perks.

## Story Blog / Story Book

The story blog should serialize the world in a way that feeds every channel:

- Character origin posts
- Power-system explainers
- Gayelle/lavway/drum lore
- Behind-the-scenes art breakdowns
- Storyline chapters
- Downloadable illustrated PDF lead magnets
- KDP book previews

## Agent Memory Rule

Every agent writes outcomes back to growth memory:

- What content was posted
- Hook used
- Platform
- Spend
- Clicks
- Leads
- Cost per lead
- Comments/fan language
- Winning creative
- Next recommended action

No agent works in isolation. Story, FX, merch, books, and growth must all share results.
