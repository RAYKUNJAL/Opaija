## Universal spec (all backgrounds)

- **No characters in frame** — these are empty environments. Pipeline will composite character refs over them.
- **Wide establishing shot** — capture the full setting, room for character to be added later
- **Aspect: 9:16 portrait (1024×1536) or 16:9 landscape (1536×1024)** — both work, pipeline auto-letterboxes if needed
- **Style: OPAIJA Caribbean Glyph-Cel 2.5D anime, painterly, hand-drawn**
- **Lighting: cinematic but neutral** — golden hour, twilight, or magic hour. Avoid harsh midday or pure night unless beat specifies.
- **Detail: rich foreground, mid-ground, distant background** — three planes for parallax depth
- Resolution: ≥1024px on long edge
